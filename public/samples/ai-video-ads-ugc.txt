One-page creative brief
This brief is the contract between the business and the production pipeline. It does not need to be
long, but every field must be filled by someone who knows the product. Show it to the person who
answers customer questions – they know the real objections the owner often forgets.

Audience

Approved claims
“Visible result after one session for most patients” – not “guaranteed white teeth”.
Forbidden claims

10. Variations, testing and what to
measure
The pipeline's real value is not one video – it is ten comparable videos and a decision.
Creative testing is where the AI chain pays for itself. A human shoot gives you one or two versions;
the pipeline gives you a matrix. But a matrix only works if you change one variable at a time and read
the right numbers. The platforms agree on the basics: test 3-5 creatives per ad group, judge after a
short window and refresh when delivery declines.
